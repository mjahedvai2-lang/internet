Privacy Policy — Gaming Network Helper

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Overview

Gaming Network Helper is a lightweight web/PWA utility for checking network conditions and displaying general, legitimate gaming setup guidance.

Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally collect names, email addresses, passwords, game credentials, or personal files.

Network checks

When you use the network test, the app makes small network requests to an external speed-test endpoint to estimate response time and jitter. The results are displayed locally in the app and are not intentionally sent to an app-owned server.

Local storage and service worker

The PWA may use your browser's cache to store the app shell so that the interface can load when offline. No advertising or tracking system is included in this app by default.

Third-party services

The network test uses an external endpoint operated by Cloudflare. Your device's normal network request metadata may be processed by that service according to its own policies.

Children

This app is not designed to request personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality changes. The updated version will be published on this page.
